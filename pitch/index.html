The Origin

Named for a Man Who Bet Everything

UDOR is derived from Eudore Fontaine, who arrived in Springfield from Quebec at age 11 unable to speak English. After 30 years swinging a hammer, he made a bet in 1933 that became Fontaine Bros Inc.

1933: The Original Bet

$900 in lumber. Pledged the houses as collateral. Nine months to finish or lose everything. Eudore finished early. Four generations later, the company does $410M in annual revenue.

2026: The Next Bet

Dave Fontaine Jr, 4th generation, is building an AI-powered venture studio inside the construction company his great-grandfather started. Same DNA. Different tools. Same willingness to bet on what you know.
