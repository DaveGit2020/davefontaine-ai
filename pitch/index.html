01 — Origin

Named for a Man Who Bet Everything

UDOR is derived from Eudore Fontaine, who arrived in Springfield from Quebec at age 11 unable to speak English. After 30 years swinging a hammer, he made a bet in 1933 that became Fontaine Bros Inc.

1933: The Original Bet

$900 in lumber. Pledged the houses as collateral. Nine months to finish or lose everything. Eudore finished early. Four generations later, the company does $410M in annual revenue.

2026: The Next Bet

Dave Fontaine Jr, 4th generation, is building an AI-powered venture studio inside the construction company his great-grandfather started. Same DNA. Different tools. Same willingness to bet on what you know.

02 — Opportunity

A $2 Trillion Industry Running on Paper

Construction has been 20 years behind on technology. AI changes that equation overnight. And we have something no Silicon Valley startup has.

The Problem

$2T industry. 7M workers. Running on Excel, email, phone calls, and paper. 500K worker shortage. Razor-thin margins. Dead last in tech adoption.

Why Now

AI is a phase change. Workflows that took days now take minutes. But builders don't trust tools from people who've never been on a jobsite. That's our edge.

Our Advantage

$410M construction company. 400+ employees. Real projects. Every tool tested on real buildings with real stakes. No startup can buy that credibility.
