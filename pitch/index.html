The Origin

Named for a Man Who Bet Everything

UDOR is derived from Eudore Fontaine, who arrived in Springfield from Quebec at age 11 unable to speak English. After 30 years swinging a hammer, he made a bet in 1933 during the Great Depression that became Fontaine Bros Inc.

1933: The Original Bet

$900 in lumber. Pledged the houses as collateral. Nine months to finish or lose everything. Eudore finished early. Four generations later, the company does $410M in annual revenue.

2026: The Next Bet

Dave Fontaine Jr, 4th generation, is building an AI-powered venture studio inside the construction company his great-grandfather started. Same DNA. Different tools. Same willingness to bet on what you know.

The Opportunity

Construction Is a $2 Trillion Industry Running on Paper

The construction industry has been 20 years behind on technology adoption. AI changes that equation overnight. And we have something no Silicon Valley startup has.

The Problem

$2T industry. 7M workers. Running on Excel, email, phone calls, and paper. Chronic labor shortage (500K workers needed). Margins razor-thin. Technology adoption: dead last among major industries.

Why Now

AI isn't incremental. It's a phase change. Construction workflows that took days now take minutes. But builders don't trust tools made by people who've never been on a jobsite. That's our edge.

Our Advantage

We have a $410M construction company with 400+ employees and real projects. Every tool gets tested on real buildings with real stakes. No startup can buy that credibility.
