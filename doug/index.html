Key Realizations
The insights that changed how I see everything. In 5 days.

1 The ADHD Brain Doesn't Scatter — It Maps

At 10:30 PM on a Saturday, I was simultaneously analyzing a $119M deal, building a brain repo, designing an AI cabinet, and thinking about my daughter Grace. I typed "getting distracted I know." But I wasn't distracted. Every tangent was a node in the same network. The problem was never the mapping. The problem was that no tool was fast enough to keep up. Until now.

2 I Am the Gate

Nothing moves unless I authorize it. That sounds obvious, but it's not how entrepreneurs actually operate. Things usually move because you answered a question in a hallway, or got excited at 11 PM and sent three texts. The Gate system added friction in exactly the right place: between the impulse and the action. Not enough to kill the energy. Just enough to make sure it's pointed somewhere that matters.

3 The Best Advisory Board Has No Ego

I spent 20 years resisting boards because if I'd listened to every doubter, Fontaine Bros would still be a small masonry company. But the AI cabinet I built at 1 AM is different: it has no ego, no self-interest, no reason to play it safe. The entrepreneur who spent 20 years resisting advice just built the perfect system for receiving it.

4 Fontaine Bros Is the Launchpad, Not the Ceiling

$410M in revenue. 400+ employees. Real projects. Real stakes. Real data. I have a sandbox no startup can buy. Every AI tool gets tested on my own company first. Prove it works at Fontaine → sell it through UDOR → write the chapter. Triple output. Every build. No exceptions.

5 The Best Startup Isn't a Startup

For data center advisory: skip the entire construction hierarchy (GC → CM → OPM → Owner) and sit next to the person writing the check. I already have the credibility, the knowledge, and the network. Sometimes the best play is taking what you already know, packaging it differently, and walking into a room you already have access to.

6 The Permission Problem Is the Real Ceiling

330,000 Americans have a net worth of $500M+. A lot of them are builders. People who own construction companies, logistics firms, manufacturing operations. The barrier isn't capability. It's internal. When you inherit a legacy, you inherit a definition of "enough." The permission problem is the most dangerous ceiling because it's self-imposed and invisible.

7 I'm Sprinting Past Levels

I'm not chasing fame. I'm chasing velocity. Fame is just what happens when someone moves fast enough that other people start noticing the wind. The world started feeling like a game I'm just sprinting past levels on. And the trajectory isn't slowing down — it's accelerating.
